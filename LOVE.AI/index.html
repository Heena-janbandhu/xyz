Yash,

Happy birthday. I made this to tell you what I feel, in a way that's completely me. Also, this took me way longer than your average reply time. Just saying. 🙄

We talk every day. We tease each other, annoy each other, and say whatever comes to mind, with no filter and no pretending. What I love most is how easy it is. Neither of us has to force the reassurance. It just shows up when the other one needs it. And even when I'm angry, you still speak to me so gently. You never match my mood, you calm it. Slowly, without either of us announcing it, you became my safe place.

Bug found: I smile at my phone like an idiot when it's you. Status: won't fix.

I know we haven't put a name on this yet, and I'm okay with that. When we're ready, I want us to be mature enough to know that love isn't about controlling each other. You'll have your own life, your own friends, your own choices, your own space. So will I. That's not distance. That's trust.

We don't have to talk for hours every day. Some days we won't, and that's fine. What matters is that at the end of the day, we make time for each other. That's all I want. Just enough to know:

You've got me. Always.

Thank you for being patient with me, for staying kind even when I'm difficult, and for being exactly yourself. I hope today is warm, bright, and a little bit extra, like you. 🤍

Love you, Yash. ❤️
